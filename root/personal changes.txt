2026-03-15

added command: carry_always

this makes it so that after 3 seconds, the bot will pick up the gnome or cola again. whatever prop you told the bot to pick up.
useful for Suicide Blitz runs where the bot drops the prop and then picks it back again once it's safe.

additional changes: made angry mob range from 250 to 600 units

made it so to remember picking up by model not by ID so if the gnome is destroyed and recreated, the bot will pick up any gnome.
